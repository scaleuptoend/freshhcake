Questions

How early should I order?

At least a day ahead for custom designs. For simple cakes, call us and we will tell you what is possible the same day.

Can I get an eggless cake?

Mention eggless in your message or when you call, and we will confirm availability for your flavour.

Can you write any name or message?

Yes. Type the message exactly as you want it, including spelling.

Can I choose my own design?

Yes. Describe the theme, colours and toppers, or send a reference photo on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

We offer takeaway and home delivery. Share your address and time on WhatsApp and we confirm the charge with your price.
